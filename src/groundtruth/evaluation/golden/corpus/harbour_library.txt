The Harbourview Public Library was founded in 1962 by the merchant Thomas Ellery.
Amara Osei has served as head librarian of Harbourview since 2015.
The library collection holds 48000 volumes including a rare atlas collection.
The main reading room seats 120 readers at long oak tables.
A renovation completed in 2019 added a glass roof to the reading room.
The library opens on Sundays from ten in the morning until four in the afternoon.
