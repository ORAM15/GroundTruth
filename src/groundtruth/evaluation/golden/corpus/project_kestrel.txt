Project Kestrel is a logistics tracking platform built by the Northwind software team.
Priya Nair leads the Kestrel engineering group as principal engineer.
The Kestrel platform stores shipment records in a PostgreSQL 16 database.
Kestrel passed its security audit in November 2025 with zero critical findings.
The beta release of Kestrel is scheduled for 14 March 2026.
Kestrel sends delay alerts to warehouse managers by email every hour.
